Privacy Policy

Effective: September 12, 2026 · First Mover Projects · Contact: hello@firstmoverprojects.com

This policy describes how First Mover Projects (“we,” “us”) handles information when you visit firstmoverprojects.com, buy digital products via our Gumroad store, subscribe to Launch Alerts, or contact us.

First Mover Projects is a faceless digital-products and systems brand. We do not publish personal identities of operators on this site. Business contact: hello@firstmoverprojects.com.

How we use information

  • Deliver products, alerts, and support.

Processors

We use reputable processors such as Gumroad (checkout), email delivery providers, and GitHub Pages (hosting). Their privacy policies govern data they process on our behalf.

Cookies & tracking

This static site does not set first-party marketing cookies. Third-party pages (Gumroad, social networks) may set their own cookies when you leave our domain.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share data only with processors needed to run the business, or when required by law.

Retention

We keep purchase and support records as long as needed for accounting, legal, and customer-service purposes, then delete or anonymize when no longer required.

Your choices

Children

Our products are for business and adult operators. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or under 16 where applicable).
